# Critical Path

The order was flexible, but completion required these evidence categories:

## Who

Source: bathroom earring back plus observation that Clara was missing the matching earring.

Meaning: Clara had entered the bathroom after claiming she stayed in the living room.

## How

Source: Dr. Ibarra's ligature observation plus Clara's silk scarf hidden in the kitchenette trash.

Meaning: Javier was strangled with the scarf and staged as a bathroom fall.

## Why

Source: bedroom blackmail envelope and bank transfer note.

Meaning: Javier had been blackmailing Clara over stolen gallery money.

## Ending Gate

The final accusation required `evidence_who`, `evidence_how`, and `evidence_why`. If any category was missing, Clara produced another lie and the player returned to investigation.
