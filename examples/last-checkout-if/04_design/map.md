# Map

## Topology

The apartment was a small three-room investigation space.

- `living_room`: start and hub. Connected to `bedroom` and `bathroom`.
- `bedroom`: connected to `living_room`.
- `bathroom`: connected to `living_room`.

## Location Functions

The living room held Clara and the kitchenette. It established her alibi and hid the scarf.

The bedroom held Javier's travel bag and the motive documents.

The bathroom held the body, Dr. Ibarra, the ligature observation, and the earring back.

## Gates

No rooms were locked. The only hard gate was the ending accusation, which required all three evidence categories.
