# Story Beats

## Playable Promise

The player investigated a compact noir crime scene, gathered contradictions, and forced a confession through evidence rather than guesswork.

## Core Loop

1. Enter or revisit a room.
2. Inspect available details.
3. Question a character about new facts.
4. Convert contradictions into evidence.
5. Test whether the final accusation can be made.

## Phase Structure

- Opening: Antonio arrived and saw the initial scene.
- Investigation: all rooms and conversations were freely available.
- Pressure: contradictions appeared as clues accumulated.
- Resolution: the accusation unlocked only after who, how, and why were proven.

## Fail-Soft Policy

No clue could be lost. Repeated room visits and conversations remained available until the case ended.
