# Supporting Cast

## Clara Voss

Javier's companion for the night and the murderer. She was controlled, elegant, and adaptive. She lied about staying in the living room, then invented new explanations whenever Antonio showed a contradiction.

## Dr. Maeve Ibarra

The medical examiner at the scene. She was precise and unsentimental. She could confirm the ligature mark and later connect the scarf to the method.

## Officer Ben Pike

The uniformed officer securing the apartment. He tracked neighbor statements, corridor timing, and scene logistics.

## Javier Morrow

The victim. He had been blackmailing Clara with proof that she stole money from a Lisbon gallery fund.
