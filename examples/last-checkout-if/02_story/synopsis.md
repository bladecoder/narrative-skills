# Synopsis

Javier Morrow was found dead in the bathroom of a short-let apartment. Clara Voss claimed she stayed in the living room after he went to wash his face. Dr. Maeve Ibarra's examination suggested strangulation, not a fall. Officer Pike gathered neighbor statements and controlled the scene.

Antonio searched the apartment. In the bathroom, he found the back of a pearl earring and noticed a ligature mark. In the living room kitchenette, he found Clara's silk scarf hidden in the trash. In the bedroom, he found blackmail material showing that Javier had threatened Clara over stolen gallery money.

Clara explained away each contradiction until Antonio could present all three proofs: she was in the bathroom, she strangled Javier with the scarf, and she killed him because he had been blackmailing her. At that point she confessed.
