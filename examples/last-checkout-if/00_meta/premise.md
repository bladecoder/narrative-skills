# Premise

Detective Antonio Luna reached a tourist apartment after midnight. Javier Morrow lay dead in the bathroom. Dr. Maeve Ibarra examined the body while Officer Ben Pike kept the scene contained. Clara Voss, who had come to the flat with Javier, waited in the living room and lied whenever questioned.

The player solved the crime by exploring the apartment and interviewing everyone in any order. Clara only confessed once Antonio could prove:

- who killed Javier: Clara had been in the bathroom after claiming she never left the sofa;
- how Javier died: he had been strangled with Clara's silk scarf, then staged as a bathroom accident;
- why she did it: Javier had been blackmailing Clara over stolen gallery money.
