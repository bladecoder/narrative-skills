# The Last Checkout

An Ink interactive fiction noir case told in first person past tense.

Detective Antonio Luna arrived at a small tourist apartment where Javier Morrow had been found dead in the bathroom. Dr. Maeve Ibarra, the medical examiner, and Officer Ben Pike were already on scene. In the living room waited Clara Voss, the woman who had accompanied Javier that night.

The player freely investigated the living room with its kitchenette, the bedroom, and the bathroom. The case could only be closed after proving who killed Javier, how the murder was done, and why Clara had a motive.
