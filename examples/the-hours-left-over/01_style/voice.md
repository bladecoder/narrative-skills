# Voice

The narration should sound controlled, observant, and slightly over-disciplined at the beginning, as if Martin had learned to file his own feelings the same way he filed incident reports. As the dream intrusions intensify, the voice may loosen, but it should never become florid or vague. Precision is part of the tension.

Use short to medium paragraphs, tactile detail, and direct declarative sentences. Let fear appear first through disrupted perception, then through moral clarity.

The cyberpunk texture should come from what Martin notices without pausing to explain it: ad fog on facades, retinal prompts, subscription infrastructure, service corridors, brand language occupying public space, and the visible divide between those who own the systems and those who live inside them.
