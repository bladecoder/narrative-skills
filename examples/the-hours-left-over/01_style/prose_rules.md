# Prose Rules

- Prioritize sensory specificity over abstract explanation.
- Keep exposition attached to active scenes, records, or decisions.
- Use technological terminology sparingly and only when Martin would naturally think in it.
- Keep the cyberpunk frame grounded in labor systems, networks, transit, kiosks, overlays, and branded urban hardware.
- Let dream imagery arrive cleanly and concretely; do not announce it as symbolism.
- End chapters on altered understanding, not on cheap surprise.
- Maintain readable sentence rhythm suitable for a suspense story rather than a lyrical prose poem.
