# Lexical Notes

- Use the corporate terms `Vigil`, `intrusion`, `drift event`, `sleep pressure`, and `restoration protocol` consistently.
- Keep public language polished and euphemistic when it comes from Nexora.
- Use occasional cyberpunk terms only when rooted in the world: overlays, municipal mesh, update kiosks, transit guidance, licensed implants, service levels.
- Keep dream language simple and physical: doors, breath, water, sheets, hands, corridors, light.
- Prefer `night shift` and `graveyard shift` only when Martin notices the old vocabulary surviving after sleep was abolished.
