# Chapter 1 Plan

- Identifier: chapter 1
- Narrative objective: make the promise of the story concrete by showing both the benefits and the fracture of a sleepless civilization.
- Point of departure: Martin starts another work cycle convinced he knows how to sort serious cases from noise.
- Point of arrival: Martin experiences his own intrusion and discovers wording that implies Vigil never resolved sleep pressure.
- POV: Martin
- Main conflict: professional control versus evidence that refuses to stay abstract.

## Beats

1. Open in a city that is still active before dawn.
2. Martin reviews incident language and notes a cluster of dream-like episodes.
3. Field interview with Dalia Ventosa after a freight blackout.
4. Dalia describes a sensory episode that behaves like an unfinished dream.
5. Martin rides the metro home and loses nearly a minute.
6. He regains awareness in front of a stranger's apartment with a phrase from an old file echoing in his head.

## Emotional progression

- Routine
- Irritation
- Unease
- Professional curiosity
- Personal fear

## Revealed information

- Vigil made sleep culturally obsolete.
- Intrusion cases are being soft-labeled as manageable drift events.
- Severe users describe dream content breaking into waking life.

## Continuity watchpoints

- Keep Martin's first intrusion brief and credible.
- Do not overexplain the science in the opening chapter.
- Make Dalia memorable enough to anchor the early threat.

## Style reminders

- Keep the city vivid but not overloaded.
- Use corporate language as texture, not parody.
- End on altered understanding.
