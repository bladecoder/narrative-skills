# Chapter 3 Plan

- Identifier: chapter 3
- Narrative objective: resolve the ethical conflict through an irreversible public act.
- Point of departure: Martin has proof but no sanctioned path to act on it.
- Point of arrival: the city enters its first true sleep in years.
- POV: Martin
- Main conflict: preserve infrastructure or preserve reality.

## Beats

1. Intrusion cases spread through public systems.
2. Martin and Ines prepare a leak and restoration patch.
3. Vera tries to stop him with the argument of social collapse.
4. Martin deploys the protocol through Nexora's update network.
5. The city visibly slows, darkens, and falls asleep.
6. Martin accepts the personal cost and finally lies down himself.

## Emotional progression

- Urgency
- Grim resolve
- Moral confrontation
- Release
- Exhausted acceptance

## Revealed information

- The restoration protocol will return true sleep quickly and chaotically.
- Nexora's central system can still reach most users through certified updates.
- The system's apparent strength depends on uninterrupted wakefulness.

## Continuity watchpoints

- Make the public consequences immediate but readable.
- Keep the ending decisive without explaining all aftermath.
- Preserve first-person intimacy even during social-scale change.

## Style reminders

- Use concrete images of the city failing softly rather than apocalypse spectacle.
- Keep Martin's decision morally burdened, not triumphant.
- End in bodily stillness.
