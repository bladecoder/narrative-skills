# Chapter 2 Plan

- Identifier: chapter 2
- Narrative objective: prove that the threat is systemic and that Nexora already knows more than it admits.
- Point of departure: Martin begins privately checking the anomaly cluster after his own intrusion.
- Point of arrival: Martin learns that Nexora intends to distribute a booster while suppressing evidence of convergence.
- POV: Martin
- Main conflict: whether truth can still be handled internally once the institution is shown to be lying.

## Beats

1. Martin pulls old records and reaches out to Ines.
2. They compare current drift events with archived trial failures.
3. Martin suffers another intrusion in the cafeteria.
4. Ines exposes buried projection files.
5. Vera frames the problem as a stability issue, not a truth issue.
6. Martin understands that compliance now means active harm.

## Emotional progression

- Suspicion
- Focus
- Destabilization
- Horror
- Clarity

## Revealed information

- Vigil redistributed rather than erased sleep pressure.
- REM-like states are fragmenting into wakefulness.
- Nexora's leadership has modeled large-scale convergence.
- The booster rollout is a reputational containment strategy.

## Continuity watchpoints

- Keep Ines distinct from Martin in temperament.
- Let Vera sound rational, not cartoonishly evil.
- Make the second intrusion more invasive than the first.

## Style reminders

- Increase tempo without losing legibility.
- Tie technical findings to scene action.
- Land the chapter on a decision threshold.
