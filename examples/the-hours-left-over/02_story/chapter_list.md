# Chapter List

## Chapter 1 - The Case Queue

- POV: Martin
- Dramatic purpose: Establish the sleepless world, Martin's role inside Nexora, and the first credible evidence that dream states are returning.
- Summary: Martin interviews Dalia Ventosa after a flagged blackout, dismisses nothing for the first time in years, then suffers his own intrusion on the metro and finds a buried research phrase pointing to unresolved sleep pressure.
- Change produced: Martin moves from professional distance to personal alarm.

## Chapter 2 - Intrusion Window

- POV: Martin
- Dramatic purpose: Escalate the phenomenon from anomaly to systemic threat and reveal that Nexora already knows.
- Summary: Martin teams up with data analyst Ines Baeza, uncovers suppressed trial records, experiences more invasive dream leakage, and learns that a booster rollout will conceal rather than solve the failure.
- Change produced: Martin shifts from investigation to active opposition.

## Chapter 3 - Restoration

- POV: Martin
- Dramatic purpose: Force the ethical choice and deliver the irreversible act that resolves the story.
- Summary: As intrusion events multiply across the city, Martin leaks the truth and injects a restoration protocol into Nexora's update network, bringing sleep back at civilization-wide cost.
- Change produced: Martin sacrifices the optimized order to preserve reality itself.
