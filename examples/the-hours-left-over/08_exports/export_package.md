## Short summary

Martin Arce, a technician at the company that abolished sleep, discovers that the human brain has not stopped needing dreams. When suppressed dream states begin leaking into waking life across the city, he must choose between preserving the sleepless order built by Vigil or forcing humanity back into real sleep.
