# Protagonist

## Martin Arce

- Age: 34
- Role: Adverse-effect oversight technician at Nexora Therapeutics
- Core function: Narrator and moral hinge of the story

## Personal profile

Martin is methodical, intelligent, and professionally desensitized. He has spent years converting human distress into manageable corporate language. He is good at seeing patterns and bad at admitting when one of them implicates the structure that pays him.

He entered the field because he believed pharmaceutical work could reduce suffering at scale. Vigil let him believe that history had finally produced a miracle clean enough to justify the compromises around it.

## Internal conflict

Martin's deepest fear is not unemployment or punishment. It is complicity. Once he knows the side effects are inevitable, every small act of compliance becomes morally visible.

## Arc

He begins as a functionary inside the machine and ends as the person who breaks it.
