# Relationships

## Martin and Ines

They share technical literacy and institutional fatigue. Ines trusts Martin enough to expose data, but not enough to martyr herself without hesitation. Their alliance is practical, wary, and brief.

## Martin and Vera

Vera mentored Martin into the language of controlled risk. Their conflict is not emotional intimacy turning sour; it is a clash between two definitions of responsibility.

## Martin and Dalia

Dalia is not a friend or recurring partner. She matters because she arrives as a witness Martin can no longer reduce to an item in a queue.
