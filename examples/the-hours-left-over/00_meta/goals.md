# Goals

- Write a complete English-language short story that demonstrates the repository workflow end to end.
- Deliver a reader-facing manuscript of roughly eight pages in standard prose layout.
- Build a story that works both as psychological suspense and as social science fiction.
- Make the world legibly cyberpunk through infrastructure, class texture, and corporate control rather than through generic jargon.
- Keep the emotional core tied to one protagonist's ethical decision rather than broad exposition.
- End with a decisive action that changes the social order, not a teaser for a sequel.
