# Premise

In a near-future world transformed by a pharmaceutical breakthrough, humanity no longer sleeps. Nexora Therapeutics has made night obsolete with Vigil, a drug that keeps the brain chemically awake without the usual crash. Cities run without pause, economies swell, and rest becomes a relic of an inefficient past.

Martin Arce works inside the machine as a technician in adverse-effect oversight. His job is to review the anomalies that corporate dashboards smooth into acceptable risk. At first the reports look minor: momentary blackouts, sensory drift, brief hallucinations that resemble dream logic. Then the incidents begin to cluster, and the people affected all describe the same impossible thing. Sleep has not vanished. It has been forced underground.

As waking life starts to fracture under the pressure of suppressed dreaming, Martin discovers that the company already knows the truth. If Vigil continues to scale, humanity will slide into a permanent mixed state where reality and dream can no longer be told apart. He must decide whether to protect the sleepless civilization built on Vigil, or destroy it by giving people back the one function they were persuaded to abandon.
