# Constraints

- Narrative voice must stay in first person from Martin Arce's perspective.
- Verb tense must remain past throughout the story.
- The story language is English.
- The worldbuilding should feel near-future and plausible rather than space-operatic.
- The style should read as cyberpunk: dense urban infrastructure, omnipresent corporate systems, and visible social stratification.
- Corporate explanation should stay legible, but the prose must avoid turning into a white paper.
- Dream intrusions must escalate across the story and become materially disruptive.
- The ending must restore actual sleep to humanity at major social cost.
