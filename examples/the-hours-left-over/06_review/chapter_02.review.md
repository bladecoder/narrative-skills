# Chapter 2 Review

## Summary

The chapter expands the anomaly into a systemic crisis, introduces Ines as an ally, intensifies Martin's intrusions, and reveals Nexora's suppressed convergence modeling.

## What works

- Ines arrives with a distinct pragmatic energy and improves scene velocity.
- The cafeteria intrusion deepens the horror without losing clarity.
- Vera's argument for stability makes the corporate position feel dangerous because it is rational.
- The transit advisory near the end effectively externalizes the stakes.

## Main problems

- The science reveal is dense and risks arriving too compactly unless the scene keeps enough bodily interruption around it.
- Martin's decision at the end should feel like a threshold crossed, not only a logical next step.

## Secondary problems

- A little more friction between Martin and Ines would sharpen their alliance.
- The booster concept is clear, but the reputational motive should remain visible so the choice is not framed as mere incompetence.

## Continuity risks detected

- Keep Martin impaired but functional enough to act in chapter 3.
- Make sure the restoration protocol is framed as chaotic and costly, not magical.

## Prioritized recommendations

1. Reinforce the physical immediacy of Martin's second intrusion.
2. End with stronger volitional pressure behind Martin's choice.
3. Preserve Vera's systemic viewpoint for the final confrontation.

## Recommended next action

Revise chapter 2 before locking the manuscript.
