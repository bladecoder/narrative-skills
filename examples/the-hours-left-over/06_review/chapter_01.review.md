# Chapter 1 Review

## Summary

The chapter establishes the sleepless city, Martin's corporate role, Dalia's case, and Martin's first personal intrusion.

## What works

- The opening creates a credible post-sleep urban atmosphere quickly.
- Dalia's testimony is concrete and memorable enough to anchor the premise.
- Corporate language and moral numbness are well integrated into Martin's voice.
- The ending line reframes the central promise cleanly.

## Main problems

- Martin's emotional shift after interviewing Dalia could sharpen one degree more before the metro scene.
- The archive note is strong, but the transition to it risks feeling slightly convenient if not grounded in his active handling of the tablet.

## Secondary problems

- Gran Via and the transit setting work, but one more detail of ordinary sleepless domestic life could make the world even more specific.
- Vera appears briefly but could carry a slightly clearer threat profile for later payoff.

## Continuity risks detected

- Keep the scale of Martin's first intrusion limited so later escalation remains available.
- Do not let the archive phrase overexplain the mechanism too early.

## Prioritized recommendations

1. Preserve the chapter mostly as is.
2. Make sure Martin's first intrusion remains visceral but short.
3. Carry Vera's institutional confidence more forcefully in chapter 2.

## Recommended next action

Continue to chapter 2 with moderate escalation.
